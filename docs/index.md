# Law by AI

AI-powered platform for legal document processing, retrieval, and analysis.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Law by AI aims to make legal information accessible, searchable, and actionable
through modern AI techniques — from document ingestion and semantic search to
change tracking and knowledge graph exploration.
